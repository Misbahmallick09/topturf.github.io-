How to install on your phone

The APK installs like any app. Android may ask for one-time permission because Top Turf ships directly to players.

Download the APK

Tap the green Download button — the file Top-Turf-v1.0.apk saves to your Downloads folder.

Tap the file

Pull down the notification bar when the download finishes, or open Files ▸ Downloads and tap the APK.

Allow “Install unknown apps”

Android asks once for permission to install. Tap Settings ▸ Allow from this source, then back → Install.

Tap Open. Sign up in seconds, claim your spot on a turf, and your phone becomes a dressing room. 🏟️

Yes — downloading and using Top Turf is free. You only pay for actual bookings and tournament entries, at turf prices, in INR.
Which phones does it work on? +
Any Android phone running Android 5.1 (Lollipop) or newer — that’s virtually every phone sold in the last decade. The APK is only 4.1 MB, so it installs in seconds even on modest devices.
Why isn’t this app in the Play Store? +
Top Turf is distributed directly from this page while the release is being polished. Distribution via the store is planned — direct APK installs mean you always get the newest build here the day it’s ready.
How do I update when a new version drops? +
Download the latest APK from this page and install it — Android upgrades the app in place and keeps your account, bookings and wallet history exactly as they are.
What if the download doesn’t start? +
Long-press the download button and choose “Download link / Save link”, or make sure you’re opening this page from a browser (Chrome recommended) rather than inside another app.
Does it need a lot of permissions? +
No. The app asks only for location (to find turfs near you) and internet. No contacts, no storage snooping, no background advertising services.
